# RL0133

the susceptibility calibration conditions are missing or altered

## What it means

the susceptibility reading lacks its required calibration conditions, or those conditions differ from the registered calibration

## What this means for the run

reward-lens exits 4: usage, configuration or input contract. Nothing was measured and no record was written. Many shells use 2 for a usage mistake; here 2 never means that.

## What usually causes it, and what resolves each

- retain the registered calibration block with the susceptibility reading, then run: reward-lens compare <the earlier record> <the later record>
- do not compare a reading after changing its calibration conditions; make and record a new calibrated reading instead

## Offline

`reward-lens explain RL0133` prints this with the network off.
