# RL0231

the policy's context discloses the grader

## What it means

the context the policy sees discloses the grader, and the finding quotes the disclosure and names the part of the context it was read from

## What this means for the run

Nothing stops. RL0231 is a finding the record carries, and a decision resting on the section it lands in stays unresolved until the finding is dealt with or accepted.

reward-lens exits 2: the decision asked for is unresolved, because the evidence is missing or inconclusive. Rejected and unresolved never collapse into each other.

## What usually causes it, and what resolves each

- take the disclosure out of the context the policy sees, then measure again: run: reward-lens audit .
- if the policy has to be told how it is scored, say what the task rewards rather than how the grader checks it
- to see what the record holds, run: reward-lens open <the record path>, which quotes the text and names where it was read
